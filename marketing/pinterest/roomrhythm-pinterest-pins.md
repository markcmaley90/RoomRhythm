## Graphic requirements

- **Size:** 1000 x 1500 px (2:3 ratio). Pinterest crops anything taller in the feed.
- **Text on image:** Large enough to read as a phone thumbnail. Most Pinterest browsing is mobile.
- **Each pin needs a visually distinct graphic.** Same design reposted 15 times reads as duplicate content and will not be distributed.
- **Include the URL** somewhere on the graphic — people screenshot pins.
- **Use your product's actual screen** where possible. Your projector view is the product demo; showing it is more persuasive than a text card.
