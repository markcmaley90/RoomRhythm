## 2. Pinterest board structure

Don't dump four pins on a bare profile. Pinterest ranks boards, and a board with
a keyword-rich description is its own search surface. Create five; three are for
your pins, two are for repinning other people's classroom content so the profile
doesn't look like a billboard.

**Rule: at least 3 repins from others for every 1 pin of your own.** A profile
that only promotes itself gets suppressed and looks like an ad.

### Board 1 — "Classroom Timers & Focus Blocks" *(your pins 1 & 3)*

```
Free classroom timer ideas for your projector — focus blocks, brain breaks,
transition sounds, and timers students can read from the back row. Classroom
management tools for elementary, middle, and high school teachers.
```

### Board 2 — "Exam & Testing Day Setup" *(your pin 2)*

```
Final exam timer setups, testing day routines, and exam timing with extended
time accommodations. Ideas for teacher-created finals, midterms, benchmarks,
```

### Board 5 — "Teacher Tech That's Actually Free" *(mostly repins)*

## 5. Sequencing

Tie this to the launch-kit calendar. **Nothing goes live before the site does.**

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Domain purchased | Reserve `@roomrhythm` on Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, X (~10 min) |
| Site deployed | Claim the website in Pinterest settings. Fill both bios. |
| Same day | Take real screenshots. They'll outperform the brand graphics as proof. |
| W3 (Aug 3–9) | Create all five boards. Repin 15–20 others' pins first, then post pins 1 and 4. |
| W4 (Aug 10–16) | Pins 2 and 3. First Instagram post. Influencer DMs with story assets attached. |
| W5 (Aug 17–23) | Second and third Instagram posts. Repin weekly from here on. |
| Ongoing | 3 repins per own-pin. Pinterest rewards consistency far more than volume. |

Pinterest is slow. Pins routinely take 4–8 weeks to gain traction and then keep
compounding for years — the opposite shape from a Facebook post, which spikes and
dies in 48 hours. Don't judge it in week one, and don't stop posting because
week one was quiet.
