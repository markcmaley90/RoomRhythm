# 09 — Trademark & Disclaimers (HARD RULES)

Posture: **use the marks with ® plus an explicit disclaimer** — the industry norm (Kaplan, Test Innovators, EdisonOS all do this).

## Rules
- **Never** put a third-party mark in the product name, a tagline, a domain, or a company name. College Board's guidelines forbid this and call out "SAT Preparation and Practice App" as incorrect usage; the correct pattern identifies the source, e.g. "Smith's App for the SAT® Exam."
- Template names follow that pattern: **"RoomRhythm's Mock Practice Timing for the SAT® Exam"** — never "RoomRhythm SAT Mode."
- Always render the ® on first use.
- Every template with a `trademark` field renders its `disclaimer` wherever the template name appears — picker card, runner header, exported admin log, and marketing page.
- Never use a testing organization's logo, acorn, or any brand graphic. Plain text links only.
- Never imply affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or partnership.
- Never reproduce official test content, questions, or booklets. RoomRhythm times sessions; it does not supply items.

## Standard disclaimer text
- `SAT® is a registered trademark of the College Board, which was not involved in the production of, and does not endorse, this product.`
- `ACT® is a registered trademark of ACT, Inc., which was not involved in the production of, and does not endorse, this product.`
- `AP® is a registered trademark of the College Board, which was not involved in the production of, and does not endorse, this product.`

## Caveat
College Board takes an aggressive public position that using its marks in connection with a commercial product is not fair use, even while the common industry practice is mark + ® + disclaimer. This is a real tension. **Get a lawyer's read before launch.** Nothing here is legal advice.

## Safe harbor
The `school_exam` and `corporate_training` domains carry zero trademark exposure. Lead the demo and the marketing with those.
